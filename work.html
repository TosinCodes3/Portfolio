Learn To Cut · 2025/2026

Client Case Study

Design system and content direction for a barber education brand.

Learn To Cut teaches barbers to raise their prices. The problem was presentation: premium training that looked like everyone else's. I built one editorial system and ran it across every surface the brand sells on: launch campaigns, keynote decks, educational carousels, lead magnets, thumbnails, community and event print. Nothing ships off-system. A five-day intensive sold out in two days. One educational carousel earned 22,000+ likes and shares. Thumbnails reached 119K views.

Trench Coat Mafia

Live at HairFestLTC × Gradient · 2025
Challenge
A keynote presented by Learn To Cut and Gradient together. The deck had to belong to both brands and be claimed by neither, so nothing on it could read as either one's house style.
Solution
Both brands work in neutral tones, so the deck went fully monochrome and let that shared ground carry it. No logos on the title, no accent colour, no decoration. Copy and images only, sequenced as one argument from mood and model direction through wardrobe, shot selection and posting plan.
Results
Delivered live at HairFest as a genuinely co-branded keynote, with neither identity dominating a single slide, and a sequence that still reads in the photos taken afterwards.

Faded Culture · 2026

Client Case Study

Partnership media kit for a barbering media and lifestyle brand.

Challenge
Faded Culture was read as a barber academy. The business was already far wider than that: a million-subscriber channel, apparel, live events and a community across four platforms. Nothing put the full picture in one place, which capped what partners thought they were buying into.
Solution
A media kit that leads with the whole ecosystem rather than any single arm. Six pillars stated up front, audience figures given room to land, and a gallery proving the culture exists offline. Near-black and off-white, no decoration, so the numbers carry the page.
Results
The whole business now reads in a single document. 1M+ subscribers, 16M+ annual views, 300K+ on Instagram and 271K+ on TikTok presented as one audience rather than four scattered counts, with the channel, apparel, community and events carrying equal weight to the academy.
