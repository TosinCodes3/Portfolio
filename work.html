Learn To Cut · 2025/2026

Client Case Study

Design system and content direction for a barber education brand.

Learn To Cut teaches barbers how to raise their prices. The challenge was presentation: premium training that looked like everyone else's. I developed a cohesive editorial design system and applied it across every customer touchpoint, from launch campaigns, keynote decks, and educational carousels to lead magnets, YouTube thumbnails, community assets, and event print. Every piece follows the same visual system for consistency and scalability.

The work supported measurable results, including a five-day intensive that sold out in two days, educational carousel content that generated thousands of likes and shares, and YouTube thumbnails that reached over 119K views.

Trench Coat Mafia

Live at HairFestLTC × Gradient · 2025
Challenge
A keynote presented by Learn To Cut and Gradient together. The deck had to belong to both brands and be claimed by neither, so nothing on it could read as either one's house style.
Solution
Both brands work in neutral tones, so the deck went fully monochrome and let that shared ground carry it. No accent colour, no decoration. Copy and images only, sequenced as one argument from mood and model direction through wardrobe, shot selection and posting plan.
Results
Delivered live at HairFest as a genuinely co-branded keynote, with neither identity dominating a single slide, and a sequence that still reads in the photos taken afterwards.

Highlighted Project

Sectioning Secrets

Lead MagnetLearn To Cut · 2025
Challenge
Sectioning is the foundation every haircut depends on, yet it is usually taught in passing. The guide had to make a technical process feel structured enough to study and simple enough to follow at the chair.
Solution
A landscape guide built around a clear step sequence, with diagrams carrying the instruction and space around each stage so nothing competes for attention. It runs on the same editorial system as the rest of the library, so it reads as part of a series rather than a one-off download.
Results
A reference barbers can work from directly, and a lead magnet that presents the brand's free material to the same standard as everything it sells.

Highlighted Project

Trending In 2026

22K+ likes & sharesLearn To Cut · 2026
Challenge
A trends round-up competing against an endless feed of louder posts. It had to earn attention on a platform where most educational content shouts to be seen.
Solution
Editorial restraint rather than visual noise. A clean system carries the reader from the cover through each cut, with structure and pacing doing the work that decoration usually attempts.
Results
Over 22,000 likes and shares, demonstrating that considered design amplifies reach without sacrificing clarity.
