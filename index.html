02 · Presentation

Trench Coat Mafia

Live at HairFestLTC × Gradient · 2025

A joint HairFest keynote for Learn To Cut and Gradient. The challenge was creating a deck that represented both brands without feeling owned by either. Leaning into either visual identity would have undermined the collaboration. Since both brands naturally work in neutral tones, I built the presentation in monochrome and let that shared visual language do the work. No accent colours. No decorative elements. Just copy and imagery, sequenced as a single narrative, from mood and model direction to wardrobe, shot selection, and the posting strategy. With nothing competing for attention, the sequence itself became the persuasion.
